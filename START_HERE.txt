MANGOSMP - START HERE

1) EXTRACT the zip first (right-click > Extract All).
   Do NOT double-click files while they are still inside the zip. The pages need the
   "css", "js" and "assets" folders sitting next to them. Without those folders the
   site looks like plain text (no colors, broken logo).

2) To just LOOK at the design on your computer:
   open the extracted folder > site > double-click index.html
   (Store, Leaderboards and Login need the real website. On your computer they will
   say "temporarily unavailable" until you deploy.)

3) To put the website online (Netlify, Turso, Discord, Minecraft server):
   follow BEGINNER_GUIDE.md step by step.
