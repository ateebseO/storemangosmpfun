Server rules

Breaking rules can lead to a mute, kick, or ban. By joining, you agree to follow them.

1

Be respectful

Treat everyone kindly. No harassment, hate speech, discrimination or toxic behavior.

2

No cheating

Hacked clients, x-ray, auto-clickers and any unfair advantage are not allowed.

3

No griefing or stealing

Do not destroy, steal from or modify other players' builds. Claim your land.

4

Keep chat clean

No spam, advertising, excessive caps or inappropriate content in chat.

5

No exploits

Do not abuse bugs or glitches. Report them to staff instead.

6

No lag machines

Avoid massive redstone clocks, mob farms or entity counts that harm server performance.

7

Follow staff

Staff decisions are final. If you disagree, calmly open a ticket on Discord.

8

Use common sense

If you wonder whether something is allowed, ask staff before you do it.

A survival server with a friendly community, fair ranks and seasonal events.
