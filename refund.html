MangoSMP Refund Policy

Please read this policy before buying anything from the MangoSMP store.

01Digital Purchases

All ranks, keys, shards, crates, cosmetics and other virtual items sold by MangoSMP are digital products.

02Refund Eligibility

  • Purchases are generally non-refundable after successful delivery, subject to any rights you have under applicable consumer law.
  • Contact support if an item was not delivered.
  • Duplicate charges and verified billing errors will be investigated.
  • Refund requests involving unauthorized transactions will be reviewed.

03Chargebacks

Please contact MangoSMP support before opening a payment dispute so we can investigate and try to resolve the issue.

04Purchase Responsibility

Check your Minecraft username and selected product carefully before purchasing.

05Support

All purchase-related issues must be submitted through the official MangoSMP Discord support ticket system.

06Policy Updates

MangoSMP may update this policy when necessary.
