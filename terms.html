01Acceptance

By using the MangoSMP website or store, or by playing on the server, you agree to these terms, the Server Policy and the Refund Policy.

02Products and Delivery

Ranks, keys and other store items are digital items for use on MangoSMP.

  • Items are delivered in-game to the Minecraft username entered at checkout.
  • Delivery happens after staff verify your payment. We aim to deliver as soon as possible.
  • If an item is missing, contact support through a Discord ticket with your order code.

03Orders and Payment

  • Prices are shown in Pakistani Rupees (PKR) and may change. Your order uses the price shown when you placed it.
  • Placing an order does not complete it. An order counts as paid only after staff verify your payment.
  • Available payment methods and instructions are shown at checkout.

04Your Responsibilities

  • Check your Minecraft username and selected products before ordering.
  • Provide accurate information and keep your order code private.
