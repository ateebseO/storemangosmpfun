MangoSMP Server Policy

These rules keep MangoSMP safe, fair and fun. By playing on the server you agree to follow them.

On this page
  1. General Rules
  2. Gameplay Rules
  3. Chat Rules
  4. Staff Rules
  5. Punishments
  6. Important Information

01General Rules

  • Respect all players and staff.
  • No harassment, hate speech or bullying.
  • No spam or advertising.
  • No inappropriate usernames or skins.
  • Follow staff instructions.

02Gameplay Rules

  • No hacking or unfair modifications.
  • No exploiting bugs or glitches. Report them to staff instead.
  • No duping or abusing game exploits.
  • No stealing or griefing where prohibited.
  • No inappropriate PvP behavior.

03Chat Rules

  • No excessive caps or spam.
  • No threats or harassment.
  • No malicious links.
  • Keep chat respectful.

04Staff Rules

  • Do not impersonate staff.
  • Do not abuse staff reports.
  • Report rule violations with evidence, such as screenshots or video, so staff can review them properly.

05Punishments

Depending on the severity and history of a violation, staff may apply one of the following:

  • Verbal warning.
  • Warning.
  • Temporary mute or ban.
  • Permanent ban for serious or repeated violations.

06Important Information

  • Rules may be updated from time to time.
  • Players are responsible for following the latest rules.
  • Staff decisions should follow fair moderation procedures. If you believe a decision was a mistake, contact us through a Discord support ticket.

Last updated: November 2026. Questions? Contact us on Discord.

A survival server with a friendly community, fair ranks and seasonal events.
